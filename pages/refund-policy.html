RISK-FREE GUARANTEE

30-Day Evaluation & Refund Policy

Acquire with absolute peace of mind. We stand behind every watch we craft.

1. 30-Day In-Hand Evaluation Window

You have 30 full days from the date of package delivery to inspect, evaluate, and admire your timepiece. If for any reason you are not completely enchanted by its craftsmanship, finish, or wrist presence, you may request a hassle-free return or exchange.

2. Condition for Full Refund

To qualify for a 100% full refund, the watch must be returned in its original unworn condition, with protective plastic films intact, accompanied by the presentation box, Certificate of Origin, and warranty metal card.

3. 5-Year Mechanical Warranty Claims

If your timepiece experiences any internal movement defect, amplitude slowdown, or water seal compromise within 5 years of acquisition, our atelier will repair, regulate, or replace the caliber under full warranty at zero labor or parts cost.

4. Rapid Refund Processing

Once our horologist inspects the returned package, refunds are issued within 24 to 48 hours to your original method of payment or direct bank transfer for COD orders.
