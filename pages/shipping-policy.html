Shipping & Delivery Policy

Every timepiece is packed in shock-proof presentation vaults and insured against all transit hazards.

1. Atelier Timing & Inspection Window

Prior to dispatch, every timepiece undergoes a mandatory 48-hour mechanical timegrapher calibration check to ensure amplitude, beat error, and rate accuracy remain within strict horology tolerances (±5 seconds/day). Orders are securely dispatched within 24 to 48 hours of placement.

2. Domestic Delivery in Pakistan (Cash on Delivery Available)

We offer complimentary insured express courier delivery (TCS / Leopard / Swyft Courier) across all major cities in Pakistan (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Peshawar, Multan, Quetta, and beyond). Estimated delivery time is 2 to 4 business days. Cash on Delivery (COD) is available at zero additional surcharge.

3. Worldwide Express Shipping

International orders are handled by DHL Express and FedEx Priority with signature-required delivery. Transit times to the UAE, UK, USA, Europe, and Asia are typically 3 to 5 business days. Full door-to-door insurance is provided at no extra cost.

4. Order Tracking & Real-Time Notifications

Once your package is collected by our courier partner, you will receive an encrypted tracking link and SMS notification allowing you to monitor the real-time coordinates of your shipment.
